### 🌐 Leading an Autonomous ISP

As the **President** of **Zenyx**, a non-profit Internet Service Provider operating its own **Autonomous System**, I drive the organization's strategic vision. My core focus is leading the team and ensuring the **high availability** of our connectivity and hosting services for all our users.

### 🛠️ Network & Virtualization Infrastructure

I oversee the end-to-end design, deployment, and maintenance of our technical architecture. This involves managing our **ASN** and **BGP routing**, setting up secure **GRE and VXLAN tunnels**, and orchestrating a mix of virtual machines and containers (**LXC, Docker, Portainer**) within a **Proxmox VE** environment.

### 💻 System Administration

Acting as the core **Infrastructure Engineer**, I deploy and maintain critical services primarily via the command line. I handle everything from our DNS infrastructure (**PowerDNS, BIND9**) and reverse proxies (**Nginx**) to maintaining robust execution environments for modern web stacks (**Next.js, Node.js, Laravel**).
