### 🎓 Cartélia: Exploring Entrepreneurship

**Cartélia** was a student-led project launched in 2024 alongside my peers to dive into the fundamentals of **business and entrepreneurship**. Our flagship product was a locally crafted, **genuine leather card holder**, and we proudly donated **100% of our profits** to a local non-profit organization.

### 🎨 Web & Digital Communication

Wearing multiple hats, I was primarily in charge of developing the **website** and designing all our **communication assets**. This included managing our social media presence (Instagram & Facebook) as well as creating physical leaflets and advertising campaigns.

### ⚙️ Technical Operations

Acting as the **technical lead** for the initiative, I fully managed the **POS (Point of Sale) system** and streamlined the digital workflow to handle both pre-orders and direct sales efficiently.
